## Part A · Presentation

### 1 · Title

From syllabus to evidence. Training and researching Integral Personal Development. We are Martha Agila from UTPL and Rodrigo Proaño from UTE. Both are universities in Ecuador. We bring faculty training and research together in one tool.

### 2 · The problem

Integral Personal Development is not taught as content. It grows through a training process that needs support from research. The syllabus should always be within reach and not only in a P D F. That is why we bring it to a mobile app. Reviewing the literature takes weeks. We lived it with ten real sources in three languages. None of them used the term Integral Personal Development. The sacral and cosmic domains were the least studied. Where should a teacher start?

### 3 · The tool

The tool is used by university teachers in training and by the person who facilitates that training. It combines two course guides. With guide twenty one the teacher uploads a syllabus in Word. The result is a course website and a mobile app. With guide eight the teacher provides sources in P D F. They receive a synthesis where every claim has a source and a page. Both paths share one progress record.

### 4 · How it works

It works along two paths. Training and research. Teachers can start with either one and their progress is recorded. In the first step training begins. The teacher sees the Human Competencies for the Future syllabus or uploads another one. In the second step A I converts the syllabus and the teacher reviews the result. In the third step research begins. The assistant proposes in five stages and quotes the literal passage with its page. In the fourth step people decide. Two reviewers score separately and approve each stage. The A I proposes and people always decide.

### 5 · Evidence

This is what we verified. In training we tested two real syllabi from UTE and UTPL. All twenty six course contents match the official syllabus word for word. In research the two reviewers reached a kappa of zero point seven zero before consensus. That counts as substantial agreement. All sixty quoted passages appear on their page. Human review also caught an overstated claim that the automatic check missed. We corrected it in version one point one. Use with real teachers is still a proposal.

### 6 · Limits and responsible use

The syllabus is sent to Claude only to convert it. It contains only institutional data about the facilitator and the site does not store it. The tool does not replace the reviewers' judgment. Progress is self reported and complements the virtual classroom. Articles are processed on the computer. Our next step is to apply it in Canvas with teachers in training.
